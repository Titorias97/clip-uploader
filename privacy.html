Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

Clip Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload), to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channels.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly), to confirm which channel was selected before saving its sign-in.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens and video details are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Clip Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Titorias97/clip-uploader.
